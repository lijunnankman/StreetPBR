StreetPBR

Dynamic Relightable Urban Scene Reconstruction from Unconstrained Photos

Junnan Li, Haozhe Xie, and Shengping Zhang

StreetPBR reconstructs dynamic street scenes as relightable PBR Gaussians from unconstrained photos. The goal of this page is simple: show whether the recovered scene can change lighting coherently while keeping geometry, materials, vehicles, and road structure stable.

DynamicStreet

A synthetic street-scene benchmark with controllable lighting and material annotations.

DynamicStreet is built with realistic ego-vehicle trajectories in Unreal Engine and provides synchronized RGB, depth, normal, material, and illumination annotations for evaluating dynamic urban inverse rendering.
